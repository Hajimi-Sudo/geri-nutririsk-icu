\section{Supplementary Methods}

All final result-lock experiments were rerun on the remote analysis server on 2026-08-26. The runtime root was \texttt{/root/}\allowbreak\texttt{autodl-tmp/}\allowbreak\texttt{frontiers\_}\allowbreak\texttt{nutrition\_}\allowbreak\texttt{mimic\_}\allowbreak\texttt{eicu}, with MIMIC-IV v3.1 under \texttt{/root/}\allowbreak\texttt{autodl-tmp/}\allowbreak\texttt{mimiciv/}\allowbreak\texttt{mimic-iv-3.1} and eICU-CRD under \texttt{/root/}\allowbreak\texttt{autodl-tmp/}\allowbreak\texttt{eicu/}\allowbreak\texttt{base}. \subsection{Supplementary Table S1: Harmonized 83-Predictor Dictionary and Comprehensive Cross-Database Distributions}
\label{sec:tab_s1}

Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:dict_s1} documents all 83 features harmonized between MIMIC-IV ($n = 33{,}671$) and eICU-CRD ($n = 71{,}861$), including clinical domain, measurement units, missingness rates, and comprehensive distributional summaries (mean $\pm$ standard deviation, median, interquartile range, and observed value ranges [min--max]), verifying cross-system comparability. All routine 24-hour EHR laboratory parameters and vital signs were summarized using the \textbf{median} value across valid measurements within the initial 24 hours of ICU admission (capturing central tendency and mitigating transient draw noise), while GCS was extracted as the first-day nadir and urine output as cumulative 24-hour volume. Extraction rules, EHR source fields, 24-hour aggregation strategies (all routine laboratories and vitals: first-day median; GCS: first-day nadir; urine output: cumulative 24h total), and missingness-indicator definitions are documented in the companion open-source data dictionary (\texttt{supplementary\_table\_s1\_predictor\_dictionary\_and\_distributions.csv}) and Section~2.2.
